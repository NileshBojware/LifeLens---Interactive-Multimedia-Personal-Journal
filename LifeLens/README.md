# LifeLens ★ Retro 8-Bit Multimedia Journal (Eternal Cosmos & Dual Mixer Edition)

> **A retro pixel-art desktop personal journal application featuring 6 dynamic personality color themes (including pitch-black retro space game *Eternal Cosmos*), Hans Zimmer's Interstellar S.T.A.Y ambient BGM, golden-brown.mp3 quote narration music, dual-track background audio mixer, live webcam snapshot capture, 365+ psychological quotes, 8-bit image filters, and speech synthesis.**

---

## 🌟 Key Multimedia & Audio Features

### 1. 🌌 App Ambient BGM (*Hans Zimmer - S.T.A.Y [Interstellar Theme]* slowed + reverb)
- Atmospheric, cosmic ambient background music automatically playing or togglable with the **`[ 🎵 AUDIO ON/OFF ]`** button.
- Adjustable volume level with live preview and track switcher in Settings.

### 2. 🎵 Synchronized Quote Reading (`golden-brown.mp3` + Real-Time Word-by-Word Highlighting)
- When clicking **`[ 🔊 READ ALOUD + BGM ]`** on any psychological quote card or journal quest log:
  - **`golden-brown.mp3`** automatically plays softly in the background under the 0.75x calm voice narration.
  - **Karaoke / Word-by-Word Highlight**: Each word lights up with an opaque theme-colored rectangle in real-time as it is spoken by the voice!
  - The main app BGM automatically ducks to maintain pristine vocal clarity.
  - As soon as speech finishes, the highlight clears and `golden-brown.mp3` smoothly fades out!

### 3. 🎚️ Dual-Track Audio Mixer & Soundtrack Settings
Control and customize all audio in **Specs / Themes / Audio Mixer** (`AboutScreen`):
- **`🎵 APP AMBIENT BGM` (0% – 100%):** Volume slider, current file name display (`interstellar_stay.mp3`), `[ 📁 CHANGE BGM ]` track browser, and `[ ▶ TOGGLE BGM ]` live audition button.
- **`🎙️ QUOTE READING BGM` (0% – 100%):** Volume slider, active file display (`golden-brown.mp3`), `[ 📁 CHANGE QUOTE MUSIC ]` file picker, and `[ ▶ TEST QUOTE BGM ]` preview button.
- All volume levels and chosen audio track paths are automatically saved in `data/settings.json`.

### 4. 🎭 6 Personality Themes (Featuring 🚀 Eternal Cosmos)
Switch on the fly between:
- **🚀 Eternal Cosmos**: Deep pitch-black retro space game with supernova cyan, pulsar gold, and nebula violet starlight.
- **🎮 Cyber Gamer**: Neon glitch cyan & arcade magenta retro cyberpunk.
- **🌿 Zen Sage**: Mindful emerald jade & matcha forest tranquility.
- **🎨 Vibrant Artist**: Sunset coral & electric violet creative passion.
- **🌌 Deep Scholar**: Analytical celestial indigo & stellar gold intellect.
- **☕ Cozy Nomad**: Warm amber & caramel fireside nostalgia.

### 4. 🧠 365+ Curated Psychological Quotes
- Daily automated quote rotation plus instant random shuffle and calm 0.75x speech narration.

### 5. 📸 Webcam Snapshot & File Upload
- Snap real-time webcam photos or upload images with 8-bit retro filters.
